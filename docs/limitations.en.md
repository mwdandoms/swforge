# Community 2.0 limitations

- Drawing, GB, review and batch functionality retain the public 1.7 scope; only necessary compatibility and reliability fixes are included.
- Springs and auger flights produce geometry-only solids without native construction history or driving dimensions. Mixed builds containing them are flattened as a whole. They remain measurable and directly editable; regenerate for parameter changes.
- Source code remains MIT. Geometry-only delivery is not encryption or a restriction against modifying open-source code.
- Explicit recipe relations are not native cross-document equations. Cosmetic threads do not produce physical thread geometry.
- See [operation-specific validation](validation-community.md). Testing two releases does not certify every operation or other versions/service packs.
- Production drawings and mechanical decisions require engineering review. No complete GB, strength or manufacturing certification is implied.
